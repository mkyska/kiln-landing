FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers before you send the first email.

What counts as “in scope”?

Whatever we write down together before kickoff: deliverables, data sources, environments, and acceptance criteria. If something new appears mid-sprint, we pause and quote it rather than silently expanding the timeline.

Who is Kiln for?

Founders and small-to-medium businesses who need a specific tool working soon — not a long retainer for vague “AI strategy.” If you can describe the job in a paragraph, we can usually scope it.

Do you take equity or open-ended retainers?

No. Sprints are fixed-price cash engagements. Keep is a simple monthly care plan after a sprint ships. We do not trade for equity or invent backlog hours.

What do I need to provide?

Access to the systems involved (or sample data if access is restricted), a point of contact who can answer questions within a business day, and written approval of the scope. Hosting and third-party API costs remain yours unless we agree otherwise.

What happens after handoff?

You own the deliverable. Included revision rounds cover reasonable in-scope fixes. If you want ongoing tweaks and monitoring, Keep is optional. We are happy to document enough that your team (or another contractor) can maintain it.

Ready to scope a build?

Send a short description of the job. We reply with questions and a draft scope — or a clear no if it is not a fit.
